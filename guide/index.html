How to Gain EXP Fast

Three simple ways to earn experience points in Monster Hunter Outlanders without spending money.

DAILY ADVENTURE REQUESTS3,600 EXPComplete your daily requests for a major EXP boost.
01
FIRST METHOD · DAILY

Complete Daily Adventure Requests

Log in every day and complete your Daily Adventure Requests to earn an outstanding 3,600 EXP points.

02
SECOND METHOD · SIDE MISSIONS

Finish Your Side Missions

Complete all available Side Mission Requests. These missions reward you with additional EXP points as you progress.

03
THIRD METHOD · FREE HUNTS

Complete Free Hunts Research Objectives

Work through all of your Free Hunts Research Objectives to earn more EXP points while hunting.

Hunter tip: Start with your daily requests, then work on side missions and Free Hunts research objectives.

Pay-to-Win EXP Guide

Premium progression options and daily resource benefits, explained with original in-game screenshots.

01
FIRST METHOD · SHOP SUBSCRIPTION

Unlock Daily Subscription Benefits

Access the in-game subscription to collect extra daily resources and support your progression.

★ $4.99 + APPLICABLE TAX

Step 1 · Find the Subscription

Open the Shop from the game menu. Once inside, use the navigation panel on the left side and select Subscription to view the offer.

Step 2 · Collect Your Daily Benefits

The subscription is listed at US$4.99 plus applicable tax. According to the provided guide details, an active subscription lets you collect 40 stamina and 100 Ruta Coins daily. Check the in-game reward screens regularly so you don't miss available benefits.

02
SECOND METHOD · DAILY STAMINA REFILLS

Purchase Extra Stamina for Daily Hunts

Extend your daily farming sessions with additional stamina from the Energy Replenishment menu.

★ UP TO 200 EXTRA STAMINA DAILY

How to Refill Your Stamina

While viewing your Daily Hunt Requests, tap the stamina icon in the upper-right corner to open the Energy Replenishment menu. You can purchase up to five stamina refills per day, with each refill restoring 40 stamina—a maximum of 200 additional stamina daily.

Each refill costs Ruta Coins, and the price increases with successive purchases. The original in-game screenshot below shows the cost of each refill.
